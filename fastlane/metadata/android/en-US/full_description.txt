PushIT is the mobile companion of the PushIT console (pushit.foxugly.com). It receives the push notifications sent by the applications you are linked to and keeps them in a clear, organised inbox.

HOW IT WORKS
• Sign in with your PushIT account (accounts are created on pushit.foxugly.com).
• Link an application by scanning its enrolment QR code, or type the code by hand.
• From then on, every notification that application sends reaches your phone.

YOUR INBOX
• Unread notifications at a glance, with an unread counter.
• One folder per linked application, with its logo.
• Open a notification to read it in full, with its creation and delivery time.
• Mark as read or unread, delete, mark everything as read, load older messages.
• Tapping a notification opens the right message directly.

YOU STAY IN CONTROL
• Unlink an application at any time from Settings: it stops sending to this device.
• Signing out unlinks the device and clears the local inbox.
• Delete your account directly in the app (Settings > Delete my account).
• Interface in French, Dutch and English.

PRIVACY
• No advertising, no analytics, no tracking.
• Connections are encrypted (HTTPS only); your session tokens are stored encrypted on the device.
• The camera is only used to read the QR code, on the device; no image is sent.
• Privacy policy: https://pushit.foxugly.com/privacy
